# Semantic benchmark status — 19 September 2026

No empirical human or authenticated-provider benchmark has been performed.
Synthetic software tests establish only the tested artifact, schema, admission
and display behavior. They do not establish coding accuracy, calibration,
annotation efficiency, latent workload or fatigue validity.

Human-coded reference data, interrater agreement, clustered intervals, correction
time and all comparative performance estimates are **not available**. Consequently
there is no evidence-based keep decision yet; the component remains experimental
and default-off. A future negative or null result is an acceptable outcome and
must be reported under the frozen evaluation protocol.
