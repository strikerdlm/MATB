# Method for a defensible MATB comparative review

The prior strategic review is useful for roadmap formation but its 8.7 versus 8.5
league table must not be treated as an empirical result. A revised comparison should:

1. Freeze implementation/version dates and use stable DOI, official URL, and
   commit-pinned source links instead of internal research-tool citation tokens.
2. Separate verified implementation behavior, author/developer reports, inference,
   roadmap items, and independently demonstrated human outcomes.
3. Report three profiles rather than one universal winner: participant-facing task
   engine, scientific evidence, and software/reproducibility infrastructure.
4. Publish every criterion, scoring anchor, evidence item, missing-evidence rule,
   assessor, and conflict resolution. Use ordinal ratings or uncertainty ranges.
5. If weights are applied, show all plausible weighting scenarios and whether the
   ranking changes. Do not interpret decimal differences smaller than scoring uncertainty.
6. Call the existing exercise an explicit post hoc decision rubric. Only a comparison
   whose criteria and weights were frozen before scoring may be described as preregistered.

The defensible present conclusion is use-case-specific: NASA supplies benchmark
lineage, AF-MATB supplies an important military psychophysiology lineage, USAARL
supplies the strongest documented adaptive-automation task-engine features, and this
repository supplies the broadest inspected open research-software and provenance
architecture. Human equivalence and superiority remain untested.
