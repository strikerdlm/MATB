# Comparative Analysis: FAA Part 107 (US) vs. RAC 100 (Colombia)
## Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS/Drones) Regulations

**Date:** March 12, 2026  
**Author:** Diego Malpica (Aerospace Medicine Physician)  
**Purpose:** Regulatory synthesis for sUAS operations, FPV, night operations, and medical certification requirements

---

## Executive Summary

| Aspect | FAA Part 107 (US) | RAC 100 (Colombia) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Weight Limit (Open Category)** | <55 lbs (25 kg) | <25 kg (Categoría Abierta) |
| **Registration Threshold** | ≥0.55 lbs (250 g) | ≥200 g |
| **Medical Certificate Required** | **NO** (self-assessment only) | **YES** (occupational medical certificate, annual) |
| **Pilot Certification** | Remote Pilot Certificate (knowledge test) | Certificado de Idoneidad UAS (theory + practical) |
| **Night Operations** | Allowed with anti-collision lights (no waiver needed since 2021) | Requires "Adición de vuelo nocturno" + specific requirements |
| **FPV Operations** | Allowed with visual observer | Recognized; EVLOS up to 3 km with observer |
| **BVLOS** | Requires waiver | Requires "Adición BVLOS" + authorization |
| **Operations Over People** | Allowed with risk-based categories (no waiver needed since 2021) | Prohibited; 30 m minimum distance from people |
| **Maximum Altitude** | 400 ft AGL | 122 m (400 ft) AGL |

## 1. Regulatory Framework Comparison

### FAA Part 107 (United States)

**Governing Body:** Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)  
**Full Title:** 14 CFR Part 107 — Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems  
**Effective:** August 29, 2016 (major updates: 2021 for night ops, 2024 for remote ID)

**Categories:**
- Single unified category for small UAS <55 lbs
- Waiver-based system for operations outside standard rules
- No subcategories for recreational vs. commercial (recreational under 49 USC 44809)

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

**Governing Body:** Unidad Administrativa Especial de Aeronáutica Civil (UAEAC/Aerocivil)  
**Full Title:** Reglamento Aeronáutico de Colombia - RAC 100: Operación de Sistemas de Aeronaves no Tripuladas  
**Effective:** January 2025 (Enmienda 2, December 2024)

**Categories:**
1. **Categoría Abierta (Open Category)**
   - Recreational and non-commercial operations
   - UAS up to 25 kg
   - VLOS only
   - Below 122 m AGL
   - No prior authorization required

2. **Categoría Específica (Specific Category)**
   - Commercial operations
   - Operations outside open category limits
   - UAS up to 250 kg
   - Requires Autorización Operacional
   - Requires Certificado de Explotador UAS

3. **Categoría Certificada (Certified Category)**
   - High-risk operations
   - UAS >250 kg or carrying passengers
   - Similar to manned aviation certification
   - **NOT YET IMPLEMENTED IN COLOMBIA** (awaiting ICAO final standards)

## 2. Pilot Certification Requirements

### FAA Part 107 (US)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Minimum Age** | 16 years |
| **Medical Certificate** | **NONE REQUIRED** — Self-assessment of physical/mental fitness |
| **Knowledge Test** | Initial aeronautical knowledge exam (60 questions, 70% pass) |
| **Recurrent Training** | Online training every 24 months OR recurrent knowledge test |
| **TRUST Test** | Required for recreational flyers only (49 USC 44809) |
| **Language** | Must read, speak, write, understand English |
| **Background Check** | TSA security threat assessment |
| **Practical Test** | **NOT REQUIRED** |

**Key Point:** Under FAA Part 107, there is **no medical certification requirement**. The pilot must only self-assess: *"Not know or have reason to know that he or she has a physical or mental condition that would interfere with the safe operation of a small unmanned aircraft system"* (14 CFR 107.57).

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Minimum Age** | 18 years |
| **Medical Certificate** | **REQUIRED** — Occupational medical certificate (certificado médico ocupacional) |
| **Medical Validity** | Annual renewal required |
| **Knowledge Test** | Theoretical exam (minimum 75% score) |
| **Practical Training** | **REQUIRED** — 20 takeoffs/landings + emergency procedures at authorized CIAC/CEAC |
| **Certificate Validity** | Conditional on valid medical certificate |
| **Additions (Adiciones)** | Required for specialized operations (night, BVLOS, >25 kg, cargo, etc.) |

**Eight Adiciones (Ratings) Available:**
1. **PBMO >25 kg** — UAS up to 250 kg
2. **Aspersión** — Agricultural spraying/fumigation
3. **Dispersión** — Seed/material dispersal
4. **Enjambre** — Swarm operations (>10 coordinated UAS)
5. **Transporte de carga** — Cargo delivery (drone delivery)
6. **Vuelo nocturno** — Night operations
7. **BVLOS** — Beyond Visual Line of Sight
8. **Instructor de vuelo UAS** — UAS flight instructor

**Addition Requirements:**
- 2-8 hours theoretical training
- 4-8 hours flight/simulator training
- Minimum flight experience hours (per regulation)

## 3. Night Operations Requirements

### FAA Part 107 (US)

**Status:** Night operations permitted without waiver since April 2021 (Operations Over People rule).

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Waiver Required** | **NO** (since 2021) |
| **Anti-Collision Lights** | **REQUIRED** — Must be visible for 3 statute miles, flashing |
| **Knowledge Test Update** | Must have passed initial or recurrent test after March 2021 |
| **Civil Twilight** | Allowed during civil twilight (30 min before sunrise, 30 min after sunset) |
| **Training** | Night operations covered in knowledge test |

**Regulatory Text (14 CFR 107.29):**
> (b) No person may operate a small unmanned aircraft system during periods of civil twilight unless the small unmanned aircraft has lighted anti-collision lighting visible for at least 3 statute miles that has a flash rate sufficient to avoid a collision.

**Key Differences from Colombia:**
- No additional certification required
- No operations excluded at night
- Self-declaration of fitness sufficient

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

**Status:** Night operations require specific authorization and additional training.

| Requirement | Categoría Abierta | Categoría Específica |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Adición Required** | NO (but limited) | **YES — Adición de vuelo nocturno** |
| **Anti-Collision Lights** | **REQUIRED** — Factory lights must remain ON | **REQUIRED** |
| **Visual Observer** | May be required | Required in most cases |
| **Cargo Operations** | **PROHIBITED at night** | Prohibited (drone delivery excluded) |
| **Authorization** | Automatic (within limits) | Requires Autorización Operacional |

**RAC 100 Night Flight Requirements (Categoría Abierta):**
- Aircraft must have factory lights (luces de fábrica) and maintain them ON during flight
- Visual observer support may be required
- **Cargo transport operations PROHIBITED at night**

**RAC 100 Night Flight Requirements (Categoría Específica):**
- Certificado de Idoneidad with **Adición de vuelo nocturno**
- Additional theoretical module (2-8 hours)
- Additional practical training (4-8 hours flight/simulator)
- Demonstrated flight experience
- Specific operational authorization from UAEAC

**Key Restriction:**
> "La aeronáutica civil aún no autoriza realizar actividades nocturnas que tengan que ver con 'Drone delivery'."

## 4. FPV (First-Person View) Operations

### FAA Part 107 (US)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **FPV Allowed** | **YES** — but does NOT satisfy VLOS requirement |
| **Visual Observer Required** | **YES** — must maintain unaided VLOS |
| **Goggles Allowed** | Yes, for pilot |
| **Simultaneous VLOS** | Required by observer (not pilot) |
| **BVLOS Capability** | No — still must maintain VLOS concept via observer |

**Regulatory Text (14 CFR 107.31):**
> (a) With vision that is unaided by any device other than corrective lenses, the remote pilot in command, the visual observer (if one is used), and the person manipulating the flight control of the small unmanned aircraft system must be able to see the unmanned aircraft throughout the entire flight...

**Key Point:** FPV goggles are permitted but the visual observer must maintain unaided visual line of sight. The pilot using FPV cannot satisfy the VLOS requirement themselves.

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **FPV Recognized** | Yes (defined in glossary) |
| **VLOS (Visual Line of Sight)** | Standard operations, up to 750 m |
| **EVLOS (Extended Visual Line of Sight)** | Operations with observer, up to 3 km |
| **BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight)** | **Requires Adición BVLOS** + authorization |

**EVLOS Concept (Colombia):**
RAC 100 introduces **EVLOS** (Extended Visual Line of Sight) — the ability to operate beyond direct visual range of the pilot but with visual observers extending the sight line up to approximately 3 km.

**Visualization Types Defined:**
- **VLOS** — Visual Line of Sight (direct viewing)
- **EVLOS** — Extended Visual Line of Sight (with observer, up to ~3 km)
- **BVLOS** — Beyond Visual Line of Sight (beyond EVLOS, requires Adición)

**FPV Racing:**
FPV operations are recognized but must comply with VLOS/EVLOS/BVLOS framework. The observer requirement applies similarly to FAA rules.

## 5. BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) Operations

### FAA Part 107 (US)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **BVLS Allowed** | **NO** — requires waiver under §107.31 |
| **Waiver Application** | Must demonstrate equivalent safety |
| **Typical Requirements** | Detect and avoid systems, observers, risk mitigation |
| **Automatic BVLS** | Not available under standard Part 107 |

**Waiver Considerations:**
- Must describe how pilot will detect and avoid other aircraft
- May require certified DAA (Detect and Avoid) systems
- Operations must remain in designated airspace
- Visual observers may be positioned along route

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **BVLS Allowed** | **YES** — with Adición BVLS and authorization |
| **Category Requirement** | Must be in Categoría Específica |
| **Adición** | Adición BVLS (2-8 hours theory + 4-8 hours practical) |
| **Experience** | Flight experience hours required |
| **Operational Authorization** | Autorización Operacional from UAEAC |

**BVLS Prerequisites (RAC 100):**
1. Certificado de Idoneidad UAS (base certification)
2. Adición BVLS on certificate
3. Certificado de Explotador UAS (for commercial operations)
4. Specific risk assessment
5. UAEAC authorization

## 6. Medical Certification — Critical Comparison

### FAA Part 107 (US)

**Medical Certificate Required:** **NO**

| Aspect | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Medical Exam** | None required |
| **Self-Assessment** | Pilot declares "no known medical condition interfering with safe operation" |
| **Physical/Mental Fitness** | Self-determined |
| **Vision** | Must be able to see the drone (with corrective lenses allowed) |
| **Substance Use** | No person may act as PIC while under influence (107.27) |
| **Disqualifying Conditions** | Self-determined; FAA may add limitations if reported |

**Regulatory Language (14 CFR 107.57):**
> (c) Not know or have reason to know that he or she has a physical or mental condition that would interfere with the safe operation of a small unmanned aircraft system.

This represents a **major regulatory difference** from Colombia.

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

**Medical Certificate Required:** **YES**

| Aspect | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Medical Exam** | Occupational medical certificate (certificado médico ocupacional) |
| **Issuing Authority** | Authorized medical examiner |
| **Validity Period** | 1 year (annual renewal) |
| **Certificate Requirement** | Must be valid for Certificado de Idoneidad to remain active |
| **Scope** | Assesses fitness for UAS operations |
| **Disqualifying Conditions** | Per occupational aviation medical standards |

**Medical Certification Chain:**
1. Obtain occupational medical certificate
2. Complete theoretical training at CIAC/CEAC
3. Pass theoretical exam (≥75%)
4. Complete practical training (20 takeoffs/landings + emergencies)
5. Apply for Certificado de Idoneidad UAS
6. Medical certificate must remain valid (annual renewal)

**If Medical Expires:**
The Certificado de Idoneidad becomes invalid until medical certificate is renewed.

## 7. Operations Over People

### FAA Part 107 (US)

**Status:** Allowed under risk-based categories since April 2021 (no waiver needed).

| Category | Requirements |
|----------|-------------|
| **Category 1** | UAS <0.55 lbs; no exposed rotating parts; may fly over people |
| **Category 2** | Impact kinetic energy ≤11 ft-lbs; may fly over people |
| **Category 3** | Kinetic energy ≤11 ft-lbs; no flight over open-air assembly |
| **Category 4** | Eligible under FAA-accepted means of compliance |

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

**Status:** **PROHIBITED** without specific authorization.

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Minimum Distance** | 30 meters horizontal from people |
| **Height Distance** | 30 meters horizontal/vertical from buildings |
| **Urban Operations** | Allowed but with 30 m distance restrictions |
| **Over People** | Prohibited in Categoría Abierta |

## 10. Enforcement and Sanctions

### FAA Part 107 (US)

| Violation Type | Potential Penalty |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Civil Penalty** | Up to $27,500 per violation |
| **Criminal Penalty** | Up to $250,000 and/or imprisonment for knowing/reckless violations |
| **Certificate Action** | Suspension/revocation of Remote Pilot Certificate |
| **Operations During Assessment** | Fines and certificate actions |

### RAC 100 (Colombia)

| Violation Type | Potential Penalty |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Administrative Sanctions** | Per RAC 13 (procedure regulation) |
| **Fines** | Economic ranges per RAC 13 |
| **Certificate Actions** | Suspension, revocation, denial of new certificates |
| **Aircraft Seizure** | Police may confiscate UAS on-site for public safety |
| **Additional Liability** | May incur criminal, civil, or police liability for damages |
| **Emergency Powers** | UAEAC can order immediate landing or retention |

## 11. Key Regulatory Differences Summary

| Aspect | FAA Part 107 (US) | RAC 100 (Colombia) | Clinical/Aerospace Medicine Implications |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Medical Certification** | None (self-assessment) | **Annual occupational medical required** | Higher barrier for pilots with chronic conditions in Colombia |
| **Night Operations** | Simple (anti-collision lights) | Complex (addition required) | Night vision concerns addressed differently |
| **BVLS** | Waiver required | Addition + authorization | Similar but regulatory path differs |
| **Operations Over People** | Allowed with categories | Prohibited (30 m distance) | Risk framework more permissive in US |
| **Insurance** | Not mandatory | Mandatory (Specific category) | Financial responsibility differs |
| **Pilot Age** | 16 years | 18 years | Developmental considerations |
| **Maximum Altitude** | 400 ft AGL | 122 m (400 ft) AGL | Equivalent |
| **Recurrent Training** | Every 24 months | Annual medical renewal | Medical surveillance in Colombia |
| **Practical Test** | Not required | Required (20 T/O-landings) | Skill verification higher in Colombia |

## 12. Clinical Relevance for Aerospace Medicine

### For Medical Certification (Colombia)

The requirement for annual occupational medical certification in Colombia creates:

1. **Periodic Health Surveillance** — Annual health assessment to maintain operational privileges
2. **Chronic Disease Screening** — Conditions affecting fitness must be assessed annually
3. **Medication Review** — Medications affecting alertness, cognition, motor function
4. **Vision Standards** — Visual acuity and field requirements for drone operation
5. **Cardiovascular Risk** — May require evaluation if condition could impair operation
6. **Neurological Conditions** — Seizure disorders, syncopal episodes would be relevant

### For Night Operations

**US Approach:** 
- Self-declaration sufficient
- Focus on equipment (anti-collision lights)
- Implicitly addresses circadian/night vision through knowledge test

**Colombia Approach:**
- Additional training module required
- May address night physiology, visual limitations
- Cargo operations specifically excluded

### For BVLS Operations

Both jurisdictions require additional risk mitigation:
- **US:** Waiver process with safety case
- **Colombia:** Structured training and certification pathway

The Colombian approach creates a defined competency pathway that could inform US waiver applications.

## Appendix C: Airspace Considerations

### FAA Part 107
- Class G: No ATC authorization needed (below 400 ft)
- Class B/C/D/E: ATC authorization required
- Restricted/hazard areas: ATC authorization required
- DC FRZ: Special authorization

### RAC 100
- Controlled airspace: Requires authorization
- Airport distances: 
  - <6 km: Prohibited within airport boundary
  - 6-9 km: Max 61 m (200 ft) AGL
  - >9 km from airport: Allowed per altitude limits
- Helipuertos: Minimum 3 km distance
