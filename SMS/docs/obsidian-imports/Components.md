[[sms]] 
[[RAC-219]] 
 1 Safety Policy and Objectives:
    • 1.1 Management Commitment: Demonstrates leadership and commitment to safety at
      the highest levels of the organization.
    • 1.2 Safety Accountability and Responsibilities: Clearly defines roles and
      responsibilities for safety across the organization.
    • 1.3 Appointment of Key Safety Personnel: Ensures the designation of individuals
      responsible for managing safety.
    • 1.4 Coordination of Emergency Response Planning: Establishes a framework for
      managing emergencies effectively.
    • 1.5 SMS Documentation: Maintains comprehensive documentation to support the
      SMS.
 2 Safety Risk Management:
    • 2.1 Hazard Identification: Systematically identifies potential hazards that
      could impact safety.
    • 2.2 Safety Risk Assessment and Mitigation: Evaluates risks and implements
      measures to mitigate them.
 3 Safety Assurance:
    • 3.1 Safety Performance Monitoring and Measurement: Tracks and evaluates safety
      performance to ensure objectives are met.
    • 3.2 The Management of Change: Assesses and manages risks associated with
      organizational or operational changes.
    • 3.3 Continuous Improvement of the SMS: Promotes ongoing enhancements to the SMS
      based on performance data and feedback.
 4 Safety Promotion:
    • 4.1 Training and Education: Provides necessary training to ensure personnel
      understand and can fulfill their safety responsibilities.
    • 4.2 Safety Communication: Facilitates effective communication of safety
      information throughout the organization.
